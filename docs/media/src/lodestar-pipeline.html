lodestar · scoring mechanics

Seven steps, in this exact order.

01
Peer group
Curated industry map, SIC-derived fallback. Nothing lumped into "Other".
02
Winsorize
At the 1st / 99th percentile, within the peer group.
03
Z-score
Against peers. Under 8 usable → sector → universe.
04
Bucket score
Weighted mean of the metrics that computed. Weights renormalize.
05
Coverage
Available metric weight ÷ total metric weight.
06
Floor
A bucket under 0.5 coverage is excluded, not scored on fragments.
07
Composite
Σ weight × bucket z, accounting penalty applied, ranked 0–100.
